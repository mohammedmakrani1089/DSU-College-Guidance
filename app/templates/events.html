STUDENT LIFE AT DSU

Explore Student Clubs

Join a club, explore your interests, develop new skills and participate in exciting events, workshops and competitions.

01

Coding, AI & Technology Club

A technology-focused club where students can explore coding, artificial intelligence, robotics, blockchain, cloud computing and other emerging technologies through learning activities, projects and competitions.

02

Robotics, CAD/CAM & Automation Club

A practical club focused on robotics, CAD/CAM and automation. Students can develop technical skills, understand practical systems and work on projects related to engineering and automation.

03

Research & Innovation Club

A platform for students interested in research, innovation, publication and project development. The club encourages students to explore ideas, work on projects and develop solutions to practical problems.

04

Creative Arts Club

A creative space for students interested in music, dance, theatre and other artistic activities. Students can showcase their creativity, participate in performances and contribute to cultural programmes.

05

Activity Club

A student-focused club for quizzes, competitions, workshops, discussions, celebrations and other engaging activities. It gives students opportunities to participate, interact with others and build confidence.

06

Sports Club

A club for students who enjoy sports, fitness and competitive activities. Students can participate in sports such as cricket, football, volleyball, badminton, chess and other games according to the opportunities available on campus.

About DSU

Excellence in Education Since 1976

Dr. Subhash University (DSU), located in Junagadh, Gujarat, is a multidisciplinary private university established under the Gujarat Private Universities Act. With an educational legacy dating back to 1976, the university offers career-focused education through modern teaching methods, experienced faculty, research opportunities, and industry collaboration.

DSU provides more than 130 academic programs across Engineering, Pharmacy, Agriculture, Nursing, Management, Computer Applications, Science, Commerce, Arts, and many other disciplines.

Our Vision

To become a nationally recognized university known for excellence in education, innovation, applied research, leadership, and collaboration while preparing students to become future-ready professionals.

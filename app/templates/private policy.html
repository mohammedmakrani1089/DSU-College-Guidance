DSU COLLEGE GUIDANCE

Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DSU College Guidance handles information collected when you use this website.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to DSU College Guidance. This website is designed to provide students with useful information about Dr. Subhash University, including academic programs, admission guidance, campus facilities, student activities, events and other student-oriented resources.

We respect your privacy and aim to handle information submitted through this website responsibly and transparently.

2. Information We Collect

You may use many parts of this website without creating an account or logging in. Depending on the feature you use, the website may collect the following information:

  • Your name, when you submit a website rating or feedback.
  • Your rating and feedback description when you use the website rating feature.
  • Information submitted through student or club-related forms, such as name, email, course, semester and selected club where applicable.
  • An anonymous browser identifier used to understand visitor activity and website visits. This identifier is not intended to identify you by name.

3. Anonymous Visitor Tracking

This website uses an anonymous visitor token stored in your browser to estimate unique visitors and website visits. The purpose of this feature is to understand general website usage and improve the student experience.

The visitor token is not your login account and the website does not require you to create an account to browse the website.

4. Website Ratings and Feedback

When you choose to use the “Rate Us” feature, you may provide your name, a star rating and written feedback.

Submitted feedback may be stored in the website database for website administration, analysis and improvement of the DSU College Guidance experience.

Please avoid submitting passwords, financial information, government identification numbers or other highly sensitive personal information in the feedback field.

5. How We Use Information

Information collected through this website may be used to:

  • Improve website features and usability.
  • Understand general visitor activity.
  • Manage student or club-related submissions.
  • Review website ratings and feedback.
  • Maintain and improve website functionality.

6. Cookies and Browser Storage

The website may use a small browser cookie containing an anonymous visitor token for visitor tracking.

You can manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Removing the cookie may cause the website to treat your next visit as a new anonymous visitor.

7. Third-Party Links

This website may provide links to official Dr. Subhash University pages and other external resources for additional information.

Once you leave DSU College Guidance and visit another website, that website's own privacy policy and terms may apply.

For official university information, students should verify current details directly with Dr. Subhash University.

8. Information Accuracy

We try to present useful and up-to-date information, but academic programs, admission requirements, fees, schedules, contact details, events and other university information may change.

Students should verify important admission and academic information through the official Dr. Subhash University website or the university's relevant department.

Visit Official Dr. Subhash University Website →

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures should be used to protect information stored by this website. However, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

This website is intended primarily for students, applicants, parents and other visitors interested in university information. Users should not submit unnecessary personal or sensitive information through public feedback fields.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, data practices or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised “Last Updated” date.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this website or information submitted through DSU College Guidance, please use the contact information provided on the website.

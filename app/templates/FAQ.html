🎓 Admission & Eligibility

Information about admission, eligibility and application.

The admission process at DSU is designed to be simple and easy to follow. First, students should check the eligibility requirements for their selected course. After confirming their eligibility, they need to prepare the required documents and complete the online registration process. Students may then need to submit their documents at the designated help centre. Students should carefully follow the course-specific instructions provided by DSU and complete all required admission formalities.

The documents required for admission can depend on the selected course and the student's eligibility category. Students should keep their academic marksheets, identity documents, photographs and other required certificates ready before starting the admission process. Students applying under a particular scholarship, reserved category or special admission scheme may also need to provide additional documents. It is recommended that students check the requirements for their specific course before submitting their application.

Dr. Subhash University offers a wide range of academic programs at Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate and Doctoral levels. The university offers programs in different fields including Engineering, Pharmacy, Computer Applications, Science, Commerce, Management, Arts and Humanities and other professional disciplines. Students can select a course according to their educational qualification, interests and future career goals. Students should check the current course list before applying because available programs may vary.

DSU provides students with opportunities to study a wide range of programs across different academic fields. The university focuses on career-oriented education, practical learning, research opportunities and industry exposure. Students can also take part in internships, projects, industrial visits, workshops and other activities depending on their program. These opportunities can help students develop academic knowledge as well as practical and professional skills for their future careers.

DSU follows an industry-oriented approach to education and focuses on connecting classroom learning with practical experience. The university promotes project-based learning, research, internships, industrial visits and industry interaction. DSU also provides opportunities for students to participate in activities related to innovation, entrepreneurship, sports and student development. This combination gives students opportunities to develop both academic knowledge and skills that are useful in professional life.

DSU offers programs at different levels, including Diploma, Undergraduate, Postgraduate and Doctoral levels. This allows students to choose programs according to their current educational qualification and career plans. The university provides programs across several academic and professional areas. Students should check the official current program list to find the exact course, eligibility requirements and admission details for their preferred program.

💰 Fees & Scholarships

Information about course fees, scholarships and financial support.

Yes. DSU provides scholarship and financial-support opportunities for eligible students. The university lists different scholarship schemes such as MYSY, Freeship Card for eligible SC/ST students, the Tuition Fee Waiver Scheme (TFWS), and scholarship support for eligible SEBC/OBC students. The eligibility conditions, income limits, scholarship benefits and required documents can be different for each scheme. Students should check the current scholarship requirements before applying.

Course fees can be different for each program and may also depend on the level of study. Students should check the fee details of their selected course before completing the admission process. For the latest and official fee structure, students should always verify the information with DSU before making any payment.

TFWS is a tuition fee waiver scheme available for eligible students according to the applicable rules. Under this scheme, eligible students may receive a waiver of tuition fees while other applicable fees may still need to be paid. Students who want to apply under TFWS should check the current eligibility conditions, income requirements and required documents before applying.

Yes. DSU provides information about education loan opportunities for students pursuing higher education. Students can explore education loans through public-sector or private-sector banks. The exact loan amount, eligibility, documents, interest rate and repayment conditions depend on the bank and the student's situation. Students should contact the university or their preferred bank for the latest information before applying for a loan.

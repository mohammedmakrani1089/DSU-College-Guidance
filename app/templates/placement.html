PLACEMENT PROCEDURE

How The Placement Process Works

DSU follows a structured placement process that prepares students and connects them with suitable opportunities.

01

Training

Students are trained for corporate culture and professional skills through the Training & Placement Cell.

02

Industry Interaction

The career team interacts with companies to understand their expectations and hiring requirements.

03

Profiles

Student profiles and relevant details are shared with participating companies.

04

Pre-Placement Talk

Companies explain their roles, profiles and projects so students can understand the available opportunities.

05

Resumes

Applications are collected and resumes of interested students are shared with the companies.

06

Shortlisting

Companies shortlist candidates considering profiles, locations, remuneration, interests and other relevant factors.

07

Interviews

The company conducts its selection process, which may include CV shortlisting, group discussion, written tests, aptitude tests and interviews.

08

Offer

Final selection results are announced by the company and communicated to the selected students.

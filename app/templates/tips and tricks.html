03 · FOR FRESHERS

Freshers Do & Don't

Starting college can feel confusing at first. Keep these simple things in mind during your first few weeks.

✓
DO

Things You Should Do

01

Explore your campus and learn where important facilities are located.

02

Ask teachers, seniors or staff whenever you need genuine guidance.

03

Participate in useful clubs, events, competitions and activities.

04

Create a regular study routine from the beginning of the semester.

×
DON'T

Things You Should Avoid

01

Do not ignore classes and important academic announcements.

02

Do not hesitate to ask questions when you do not understand something.

03

Do not wait until the last moment to complete assignments or prepare for exams.

04

Do not miss useful opportunities simply because you are nervous or unsure.

05 · EXAM PREPARATION

Exam Preparation

Proven revision techniques, logistics checks, and wellness habits to excel in semester examinations.

01
Strategic Syllabus Revision

Target high-weightage topics, key derivations, and core formulas with short summary sheets.

02
Solve Previous Papers

Attempt past university question sets against the clock to master pacing and neat presentation.

03
Index Chapter Notes

Organize flowcharts, handwritten formulas, and lab summaries for rapid last-minute recall.

04
Check Exam Logistics

Verify hall tickets, block seating, reporting times, and allowed tools before leaving home.

05
Rest, Hydration & Calm Focus

Get 7+ hours of sleep and stay hydrated so your cognitive speed stays peak in the exam hall.

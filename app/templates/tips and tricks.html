College Life Tips

Make the most of your college experience by staying active, building good connections and exploring opportunities around campus.

03 · FOR FRESHERS

Freshers Do & Don't

Starting college can feel confusing at first. Keep these simple things in mind during your first few weeks.

✓
DO

Things You Should Do

01

Explore your campus and learn where important facilities are located.

02

Ask teachers, seniors or staff whenever you need genuine guidance.

03

Participate in useful clubs, events, competitions and activities.

04

Create a regular study routine from the beginning of the semester.

×
DON'T

Things You Should Avoid

01

Do not ignore classes and important academic announcements.

02

Do not hesitate to ask questions when you do not understand something.

03

Do not wait until the last moment to complete assignments or prepare for exams.

04

Do not miss useful opportunities simply because you are nervous or unsure.
